Overview

Jet Hero was created during my time at Kiwise Digital Agency in Auckland: a hybrid 2D arcade game for smartphones, built with the Cocos2d-JS engine. You pilot a jet, dodge traffic and obstacles, collect power-ups and climb a global high-score table. We were two developers plus a graphic designer: I owned the core gameplay – collision detection, obstacle logic, power-up effects (four seconds each: slow enemies, speed up or shrink the jet, fly over, shoot, bonus points) and scoring – and co-designed the experience, while my teammate built the SQL back end and the leaderboard. Controls work with arrow keys on a PC or on-screen arrows on mobile. Once published, it reached more than 1,000 downloads.
